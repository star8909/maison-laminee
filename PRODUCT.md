# Maison Laminée

A one-shop bakery on Rue des Martyrs, Paris 9e, that laminates all-butter croissants
by hand. This is a demonstration brand: the shop, its prices and its oven times are
sample content to be replaced by a real client's.

## Users

People nearby or visiting Paris who want a croissant worth the walk. They arrive on
a phone more often than not, usually deciding whether to go now.

## What the site must do

- Show the craft: the film of dough becoming a croissant.
- Say how long it takes: three days from dough to counter.
- Give today's prices.
- Say when and where: opening hours, and when the next tray leaves the oven.
- Send the visitor on their way: directions.

## Brand commitments

- The name means laminated; it is the identity and is set as large as the page allows.
- Warm black ground, white ink, one gold accent for the action.
- No invented reviews, press, customers or statistics.

## Platform

Static web page, mobile first, built with Vite, GSAP ScrollTrigger and Lenis.
