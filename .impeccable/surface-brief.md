# Surface brief: index.html (landing page)

Mode: Persuade. A visitor to a single-shop bakery site decides whether to walk to
Rue des Martyrs and what to buy when they get there.

- Audience: people in or visiting Paris 9e looking for a croissant worth the walk.
- Job: show the craft (film), the time it takes (process), the prices (counter),
  and when and where (visit, with the next tray out of the oven).
- Primary action: get directions.
- Constraints: demonstration brand; prices and tray times are sample content for
  the client to confirm. No invented reviews, press or customers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: "Laminée" means laminated, so the name itself is the monument, cut as wide
as the screen under a film of dough becoming a croissant. It refuses the category
default of a cream page, an italic serif headline and a centred tagline.

OWN-WORLD: warm black ground #0c0a08 with a brown-black surface step #181512,
white ink, one gold #c9a45a reserved for the action (the button, the directions
row). Imbue condensed capitals for the name and display rows, Albert Sans for
everything read. Square corners, no rules, no shadows, no labels above headings.
The footer inverts ground and ink (white, warm black) so the name returns once
more at full width, as the direction sheet sets it.

STORY: the visitor watches the dough turn into a croissant, learns it takes three
days, reads today's prices, sees when the next tray leaves the oven, and leaves
with directions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the film fills the top 58% (the dough ball). In the dark band below,
one row: a single sentence of what this is at the left, the gold "Get directions"
button at the right. Under it, MAISON LAMINÉE spans margin to margin on one line,
its baseline near the bottom edge.

FORM: name-as-monument, the third of the three directions taste-skill dealt for this
brief, chosen because the client's name carries the idea. Impeccable's own roll
did not run: its launcher downloads and executes a binary, which was not run in
this session. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
